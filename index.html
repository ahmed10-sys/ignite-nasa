Blind Holdout Validation (2019–2025)

Calibrated strictly on 2012–2018 overlap years (αm), then tested blind on 2019–2025 against actual MODIS observations.

1. False-Positive Filtering: Isolates vegetation fires (type=0) & removes industrial gas flares + low-confidence noise.

2. Spatial Co-Aggregation: Maps 375m VIIRS & 1km MODIS onto a unified 0.01° × 0.01° daily grid.

3. Monthly Calibration (αm): Normalizes VIIRS sensitivity & overpass differences into MODIS-equivalent units.
